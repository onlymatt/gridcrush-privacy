1. Data Controller

GRIDCRUSH – Block Puzzle (the “Application”) is developed and operated by Mattia Santitto, an independent developer publishing under the name onlymt. The Data Controller is Mattia Santitto, whose contact address is via Strada C1 n°2, Riposto (CT), Italy. For privacy inquiries: onlymt.st@gmail.com.

2. About the Application

GRIDCRUSH is a free, ad-supported Android puzzle game. It does not require an account, registration, a name or an email address to play. Some information is stored locally, while third-party providers may process technical data and identifiers as described below.

3. Categories of Data

Data stored locally

The Application stores scores and high scores, gameplay statistics, saved game progress, settings, and Analytics/Crashlytics consent preferences on the device. GRIDCRUSH does not operate user cloud accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To display banner, interstitial and rewarded ads, Google AdMob may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers where available and permitted, app and device technical information, impressions, clicks, diagnostics and anti-fraud signals. GRIDCRUSH does not request precise GPS location. Actual processing depends on Google’s settings and policies.

Optional analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When enabled, analytics can include game start/end, scores, duration, moves, cleared lines, combos, power-up usage and advertising-related interactions, along with app-instance identifiers and technical data collected by the SDK. The game does not intentionally send names or email addresses in events.

Optional diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

When enabled, diagnostics may include crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating system, app version and installation identifiers.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases

Locally stored gameplay information supports requested game functions (performance of the requested service, where applicable). Optional Analytics and Crashlytics processing relies on consent where required. AdMob supports the free game through advertising; where required, consent for non-essential advertising technologies and processing is managed through Google UMP. Certain advertising, security and fraud-prevention purposes may rely on other applicable legal bases, as explained in the provider information and consent message. Further processing may be necessary to comply with legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application uses Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Android platform services including Google Play Services. See each provider’s privacy policy for data collection, disclosure and retention. Providers may act as independent controllers for certain processing activities.

6. Consent and Privacy Preferences

In Settings → Privacy & Data, users can independently change optional Analytics and Crashlytics consent. When required, advertising consent is handled through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), allowing users to accept, reject or customize available choices. When UMP requires a review entry point, “Advertising Preferences” is available in the same settings section. Rejecting consent does not necessarily remove all ads; non-personalized or other legally permitted ads may still be served. Withdrawing consent does not automatically erase previously transmitted data.

7. Data Retention

Local data

Local information is kept until removed through available app features, Android clear-app-data settings, or uninstalling the Application. Android backup settings may preserve or restore copies.

Google Analytics for Firebase

GRIDCRUSH’s Analytics property is configured for 2 months of event-data retention and 14 months of user-data retention, with user-data retention reset upon new activity. These settings do not necessarily apply to standard aggregated reports.

Firebase Crashlytics

According to Firebase documentation, crash-related data and associated identifiers are retained for approximately 90 days, subject to the provider’s deletion procedures.

Google AdMob

Advertising retention periods vary according to Google’s processing purposes and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Deletion and Requests

Users may reset in-app statistics, clear local app data through Android settings, uninstall GRIDCRUSH, withdraw optional consent, or contact onlymt.st@gmail.com about personal data. GRIDCRUSH does not provide user accounts to delete. Deleting local data does not automatically erase information already handled by third parties. Requests are addressed within available technical capabilities and applicable legal requirements.

9. International Transfers

Google services may involve data processing outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are subject to recognized mechanisms and safeguards such as adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses. Refer to the providers’ documentation for details.

10. Security

Reasonable security measures are used, and providers apply their own safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s Privacy

GRIDCRUSH is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not designed or promoted for younger children. The Application does not currently have an age-verification mechanism. Parents or guardians can raise concerns using the contact address.

12. Your Rights

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where available, and object where applicable; you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact onlymt.st@gmail.com. Requests are normally answered within one month, subject to lawful extensions. You may complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority or your competent supervisory authority.

This notice may change to reflect updates to the Application, providers or applicable laws. Revisions will be posted here with an updated date.
